      Machine Learning Reproducibility Challenge

      A NeurIPS 2026 Track

      Dec 2026, Sydney, Australia

      MLRC 2026

      Welcome to the home of ML Reproducibility Challenge. This is an annual event promoting research into reproducibility of Machine Learning literature. (v1, v2, v3, v4, v5, v6, v7, v8). This conference is a unique venue in the Machine Learning community to share, disseminate and discuss reproducible methods and tools, investigate reproducibility of papers accepted for publication at top conferences, and test generalizability of scientific findings by adding novel insights and empirical results.

      • 🔔 The 16 accepted papers for MLRC 2026 are announced! See the Accepted Papers below, and join us at the poster session on Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 5:00-8:00 PM Sydney time.
      • 🔔 Submissions for MLRC 2026 are now closed (the hard deadline was September 30, 2026). Thank you to everyone who submitted!
      • 🔔 MLRC 2026 will be an official track at NeurIPS 2026! Check out our Call for Papers for more details.

      Accepted Papers

      We are delighted to announce the 16 papers accepted to the Machine Learning Reproducibility Challenge (MLRC) 2026, an official track of NeurIPS 2026. The papers will be presented in person at NeurIPS 2026 in Sydney, Australia (December 6–13, 2026), alongside papers from the Main Track and the Evaluations & Datasets Track. Each paper was first accepted at the Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR), and subsequently underwent a light compatibility review by the MLRC committee to confirm its suitability for the track.

      This announcement was also published on the NeurIPS blog.

      The MLRC poster session will be held on Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 5:00-8:00 PM Sydney time. We invite all attendees to visit the poster presentations, meet the authors, and engage in discussion with them.

      The Best Paper Award goes to “Evaluating the evidence trace of NeurIPS 2025 contributions with agentic code auditing”. Outstanding Paper Awards go to “Min-p, Max Exaggeration: A Critical Analysis of Min-p Sampling in Language Models” and “A Reproduction Study of Weight-Based Mechanistic Interpretability in Bilinear MLPs”. The award winners are also marked in the list below.

      Venue

      We are excited to announce that MLRC 2026 will be an official track at NeurIPS 2026! This marks the first time MLRC is integrated as a dedicated track within NeurIPS. Accepted papers will be presented at NeurIPS 2026 in Sydney, Australia, December 6–13, 2026. As a track within NeurIPS, MLRC is a complementary venue to the Main Track and the Evaluations & Datasets Track.
